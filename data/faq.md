# Erasmus Office FAQ (fictional demo content)

## F1: What is the deadline for the insurance document?
Send proof of insurance at least 30 days before your stay starts. It must cover your whole stay.

## F2: What is the deadline for the Learning Agreement?
For spring semester stays, send the signed Learning Agreement by 15 December 2026.

## F3: What is the deadline for the transcript?
For spring semester stays, send your transcript by 15 December 2026.

## F4: When do I send the Certificate of Arrival?
Send it within 7 days of arriving at your host university.

## F5: Which documents do I need before I leave?
You need a signed Learning Agreement, a transcript and proof of insurance covering your whole stay.

## F6: What format should my documents be in?
PDF is preferred. A clear, complete photo is also accepted.

## F7: What grades do I need to apply for Erasmus?
You need an average of at least 6 out of 10 in your last semester. Some universities ask for a higher grade.

## F8: Can I apply if I failed a subject?
No. You can't apply with a failed subject from your last semester. You can apply in a later round once you have passed it.

## F9: How do I apply for Erasmus?
Email the Erasmus office with your last semester average, whether you failed any subject, your field of study, your English level and the countries you prefer. You can attach your transcript.

## F10: What English level do I need?
Most partner universities ask for B1 or B2. A few ask for C1.

## F11: Can I choose which university I go to?
Yes. The office sends the options that fit your profile and you choose which to apply for. A place isn't guaranteed, because places are limited.

## F12: What if I think my eligibility decision is wrong?
Reply to the email with the correct details and the office will look again. You can also book a meeting with the office.
